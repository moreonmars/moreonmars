# Denys Vasyliuk

I design and build websites and iPhone apps, from the first sketch to launch, and hand them over so the client can update them on their own. Studio [MOONAGE](https://moonage.me), Essen, Germany.

Before code, I spent twenty years running projects at non-profits and social businesses in Ukraine. So I build what a team actually needs.

## Selected work

- [Ira Kharlamova](https://moonage.me/work/ira-kharlamova): portfolio of a performance artist, with an admin panel she runs herself. Code: [ira-site](https://github.com/moreonmars/ira-site)
- [NGO MARTIN-club](https://moonage.me/work/martin-club): website of a Dnipro non-profit that has helped children and women for 26 years.
- [Leben in Deutschland. Test](https://moonage.me/work/leben): a free iPhone app for the German citizenship test. No ads, no subscriptions.
- [Bonlo](https://moonage.me/work/bonlo): an iPhone app for receipts and a shopping list.

Most client and app code lives in private repositories. I'm happy to walk you through it on a call.
